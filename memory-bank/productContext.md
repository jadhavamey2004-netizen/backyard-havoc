# Backyard Havoc - Product Context

## Purpose & Why This Project Exists
Traditional keepy-uppy games suffer from mechanical repetition: players only balance a ball vertically until they fail, leading to short session times and rapid churn. Conversely, classic physics destruction games (like *Peggle* or *Angry Birds*) rely on static turn-based launches.

*Backyard Havoc* bridges these worlds into a continuous active gameplay loop:
- **Momentum + Destruction**: You aren't just surviving; you are charging kinetic energy to execute precision trickshots that obliterate suburban environments.
- **Dynamic Comedy & Chaos**: Every destruction event provokes contextual comedic reactions from animated neighborhood NPCs, transforming physics interactions into an emergent cartoon comedy.

## The 4-Stage Core Behavioral Loop

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        CORE BEHAVIORAL PIPELINE                        │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
    │
    ▼
1. KEEPY-UPPY JUGGLING
   • Precise vertical timing taps beneath falling ball.
   • Escalates Combo Multiplier C (C = 1 -> 2 -> 3 -> ...).
   • Increases particle FX trail intensity and raises audio pitch semitones.
    │
    ▼
2. HORIZONTAL DEFLECTION & TRICKSHOTS
   • Angle tap offsets to bank high-C projectile horizontally.
   • Target high-value destructibles (windows, flowerpots, grills, greenhouses).
    │
    ▼
3. ENVIRONMENTAL CASCADES & NPC REACTIONS
   • Shattered props trigger secondary chain reactions (falling pots, grill fires).
   • Physics telemetry dispatches event to Edge AI / NPC engine (<200ms).
   • Animated comic dialogue bubbles trigger over reactive NPCs (e.g. Grumpy Kevin).
    │
    ▼
4. METAGAME CASH-IN & UPGRADES
   • Destruction Score scaled by Peak Combo Multiplier converts to "Street Cred".
   • Unlock ball variants (Superball, Wet Sponge, Flaming Ball) & new environments.
```

## Session Pacing & Progression Curve

### 1. Mechanical Onboarding (Minute 0:00 - 1:00)
- **Focus**: Pure vertical survival physics and tap timing calibration.
- **Environment**: Mostly static baseline backyard; generous hitboxes.
- **Feedback**: Ascending melodic chime pitch per juggle, growing motion streak.

### 2. Destruction Escalation (Minute 1:00 - 3:00)
- **Focus**: Introducing horizontal destructibles and domino chains.
- **Interaction**: A slight angular kick banks the ball into the neighbor's greenhouse.
- **Collateral Cascade**: Glass shatters $\rightarrow$ flowerpot drops $\rightarrow$ sleeping dog wakes and barks $\rightarrow$ BBQ grill topples $\rightarrow$ localized grease flare-up explosion.

### 3. High Mastery & Trickshots (Minute 3:00+)
- **Focus**: High-speed bank shots, narrow ricochet corridors, moving targets (e.g. robotic lawnmowers, rotating satellite dishes).

## Retention & Engagement Mechanisms

### The Neighborhood Hitlist (Daily Seeded Mode)
- **Mechanic**: Daily deterministic layout seed generated globally.
- **Challenges**: Fixed trickshot goals (e.g., "Bank off the garden gnome into the 2nd-story bathroom window with $C \ge 5$").
- **Social**: Global asynchronous daily leaderboards for highest score and fewest kicks.

### Target Performance Metrics (KPIs)
- **Day 1 Retention (D1)**: Target $\ge 45\%$ (Kill threshold $< 28\%$).
- **Runs Per Active User (RPAU)**: Target $\ge 6.5$ runs/day.
- **Average Session Length (ASL)**: Target 8–12 minutes.

## Ethical Monetization Model
- **No Pay-To-Win & No Energy Timers**: Skill and physics mastery are 100% uninhibited.
- **Cosmetic Customization**:
  - Ball Skins (Disco Ball, Eyeball, 8-Bit Pixel Cube, Golden Trophy).
  - Kick Trails (Rainbows, Electric Plasma, Flame Jets).
  - Pop-Art Impact FX ("KAPOW!", "CRASH!", "BOOM!").
- **Themed Expansion Packs**:
  - Haunted Victorian Yard, Retro Sci-Fi Lunar Base, Luxury Yacht Marina.
  - Bundled with unique prop sets, NPC voicebanks, and environment-specific physics triggers.
