# Phase 13 Production-Video Defect Matrix

This matrix carries forward the visual defects listed in the Phase 13 brief. “Observed” means the defect was called out in the production-video audit; root-cause entries below are code-backed where stated and otherwise remain working hypotheses. Phase 13 measures feasibility and captures baseline evidence; it does not attempt to complete the later remediation phases.

## Phase 13 evidence boundary

The current production output remains the Canvas baseline. Phase 13 adds a representative authored-source scene, renderer parity instrumentation, stable captures, and measured bundle/performance evidence. Entries assigned to Phases 14–18 remain open unless an independently reproduced defect is recorded in `.ai/CROSS_PHASE_REMEDIATION_LOG.md`.
