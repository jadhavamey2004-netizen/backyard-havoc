Apply only fixes authorized by the active phase or an explicit external-review correction. Audit cross-phase impact and inherited tests, document necessary remediation, and preserve prior approved behavior. Do not use a new phase to redesign systems outside scope, force renderer migration, or alter gameplay. Keep rationale and evidence concise so later phases can retrieve the decision without reopening entire reports.
